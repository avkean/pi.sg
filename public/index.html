How does this work?

Pi compresses your link, just like a zip file compresses files. When someone opens it, Pi unpacks it to get the original address.

Extra compression uses the server's computing power to try making your link even smaller. Your link gets sent to the server, but isn't stored. If you'd rather keep everything in your browser, you can switch it off.

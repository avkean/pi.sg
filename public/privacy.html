Privacy & security

Pi doesn't keep a history of the links you make or open.

Your links

Pi has no accounts, cookies, analytics, or link database.

With extra compression on, your browser sends the link to Pi's server. The server compresses it in memory, returns the result, and does not save the original. Turn extra compression off to compress the link entirely in your browser.

The original address is inside the Pi link itself. When someone opens it, Pi unpacks the address in memory and shows it before they continue.

Logs

Pi's app and web proxy do not keep access logs, so they do not record the links you submit or open, your IP address, or details about your browser.

Pi uses Bunny CDN to deliver and protect the site. It is configured with access and extended logging off, no log forwarding, and IP anonymisation on.

Bunny still handles the network data needed to serve each request under its privacy policy. Pi adds no analytics or tracking.

Link safety

Every Pi link shows where it goes before you continue. Pi does not visit or scan the destination, so the preview is not a safety check.

Pi links are compressed, not encrypted, so anyone with the link can unpack it. Pi only handles the address and does not host, control, or endorse the site at the other end. What you create, share, or open is up to you.
